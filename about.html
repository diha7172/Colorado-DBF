About the team

A student-run competition team at CU Boulder

Colorado Design Build Fly designs, analyses, manufactures, tests and flies a purpose-built aircraft each season for the AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition. Students lead every stage, from requirements through flight test, outside of class.

Who we are

A competition club, first and foremost

Every year AIAA publishes new rules. New rules mean new requirements, a new design and a new plane. That cycle is the whole club: we read the rules in August, fly the result in April, and start over.

Members come from aerospace, mechanical and plenty of other majors, first year through graduating senior. Most arrive with no CAD, electronics or aerodynamics experience and learn at least one of them in their first semester.

Requirements to flight test, then back again

Subteams

How the team is organised

The Project Manager and Chief Engineer run the program. Four technical subteams each own part of the aircraft, and the Treasurer keeps the money and sponsors in order. Pick the one that sounds like you, or try a couple before you settle.

Aerodynamics
01

Aerodynamics

Designs and analyses the lifting surfaces, aircraft dynamics and propulsion system to get the most out of the whole aircraft. Airfoil selection, drag polars, sizing, stability and control, propeller and motor matching, and the mission simulation that tells us what to build. Also owns the proposal and design report.

Leads: Damien and Jay

XFLR5MATLABFusion 360CFDPython
You would fit here if You like the maths behind why things fly. Coding experience is a plus, the aero leads are actively looking for it.
Structures and Mission
02

Structures and Mission

Designs the internal and external structure of the aircraft for weight and serviceability, and the internal layout and systems specific to each year's competition missions: payload bays, release mechanisms and loading procedures. The two jobs are tied together, so they sit on one subteam.

Leads: Justin Yaung and Ryan Aba Riebli

Fusion 360FEACompositesLaser cutting3D printing
You would fit here if You want to hold the thing you designed and know exactly why it does not break, or you read the rules and start thinking about how to score more points.
Avionics and Telemetry
03

Avionics and Telemetry

Designs the systems that record flight-test data, return real-time telemetry and operate everything else on the aircraft: control system, wiring harness, PCB design and radio links. That data goes straight back into the aerodynamic design.

Leads: Kareem Ahmed

ArduinoPCB designSolderingTelemetry
You would fit here if You want to build electronics that have to work the first time, at altitude, in wind.
Manufacturing
04

Manufacturing

Turns the CAD into an aircraft. Foam prototypes first, then the fuselage prototype, then the competition airframe: laser-cut and 3D-printed parts, carbon-fibre layups, integration of the propulsion, avionics and mission systems, bench checks, and the ground and flight test campaign.

Leads: Dariel Morales, Tyler Brining and Dinuka Hannadige

Shop toolsCompositesLaser cutting3D printingFlight test
You would fit here if You would rather be in the shop than at a desk, and you want to be on the flight line when it flies.
Finance and Outreach
05

Finance and Outreach

Tracks program spending, manages sponsor relationships and external funding, and runs the team's social presence and outreach on campus.

Leads: Aadhit Gopinath

BudgetingSponsor outreachSocial mediaEvents
You would fit here if You want the team to exist next year too, and you are good at talking to people.
