Everything important goes on the team calendar. Subscribe once and every full team meeting, subteam session and test day shows up on your phone.

Weekly

Full team meetings

Held every week to go over the larger items and get the whole team together for building and testing. This is the best place to show up if you are new.

Weekly

Subteam meetings

Where the subteam work actually gets done. Usually once a week, often right after the full team meeting.

Weekly

Leads meetings

Leads and advisors meet once a week to steer the program and keep the season on schedule.

Through the season

What a year with DBF looks like

September

Kickoff and recruiting

Find us at the engineering involvement fairs and the first full team meeting of the year. Rules drop around the same time, so you arrive right as the design starts.

October to December

Design reviews

Preliminary and detailed design reviews with our faculty advisors. Partners are welcome to sit in.

January to March

Build days and flight tests

Weekend build sessions in the shop, then test flights at the field once the airframe is ready. Bring a jacket.

April

Competition trip

The team travels to the AIAA fly-off. The 2027 competition is in Tucson, Arizona, 15 to 18 April.

Year round

Outreach

Tabling on campus, demos for prospective students and the occasional pizza night with our sponsors.
