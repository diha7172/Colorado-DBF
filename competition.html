The competition

AIAA Design/Build/Fly

A full aircraft development cycle compressed into one academic year. Teams submit a written proposal, publish a design report, then fly the aircraft they built against a scored mission profile that changes every year.

What it takes

Competitive at every stage

Students must balance aerodynamic performance, structural margin, weight, manufacturability, reliability and mission requirements, and then demonstrate the result in flight, in front of judges.

Proposals are screened, design reports are scored, and only aircraft that clear technical inspection are allowed to fly. Final placement combines the report score with the flight scores from each mission.

  • Proposal · short written pitch that earns an invitation
  • Design report · the scored engineering document
  • Technical inspection · safety and rules compliance before any flight
  • Flight missions · usually a test flight, a payload mission and a mission built around the year's theme

Season timeline

One aircraft per season, requirements to flight test

The 2026 to 2027 season ends at the 31st AIAA Design/Build/Fly fly-off in Tucson, Arizona. Here is how the year breaks down.

Build season
Late August to September

AIAA releases the rules

The mission profile changes every year. The whole team reads the rules together and starts pulling apart what actually scores points.

September

Mission analysis

Sensitivity studies in MATLAB decide what matters most: payload, speed, laps or weight. This sets the design targets for every subteam.

October

Preliminary design and proposal

Concept trades, first sizing and a written proposal to AIAA. Proposals open mid-October and are due at the end of the month.

November

Invitations to report

AIAA screens the proposals and invites teams to submit a full design report. Notifications land around the third week of November.

November to December

Detailed design

Full CAD in Fusion 360, structural analysis, propulsion testing and the first manufacturing of components.

January to February

Build, test and refine

Airframe layup, avionics integration, ground testing and the first flights of the season. Results feed straight back into the design.

February

Design report

The scored written report is submitted in the first three weeks of February. It counts toward the final placement.

March to April

Finalizing

Flight-test campaign, mission rehearsals, technical inspection prep and packing the aircraft for the trip.

April 15 to 18, 2027

Fly-off · Tucson, Arizona

Technical inspection, then scored missions in front of judges. The 31st AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition.
